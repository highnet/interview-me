# Question bank

Pick from this; don't run through it in order. Rephrase every question using the user's own words and whatever you've already learned. Sources are in brackets: MT *Mom Test*, IU *Interviewing Users*, ER *Exploring Requirements*, CH *Coaching Habit*, NS *Never Split the Difference*.

## Openers
- What's on your mind with this? [CH]
- What are you hoping becomes possible? (What progress are you trying to make?)
- What happened recently that made you want this? [MT]

## 1. Core
- In one sentence, what is this? Here's my draft. Fix it.
- Who is it for? Who is it definitely *not* for? [ER]
- What do they do today instead? What's painful about that? [MT]
- If only one thing ships, what is it?

## 2. The real challenge
- What's the real challenge here for you? [CH]
- What would this let you do that you can't do now? [MT]
- What does solving it unlock? What's it worth? [ER]
- And what else? Ask again until nothing new comes up. [CH]

## 3. Picture
- Walk me through the last time you wished this existed. [MT]
- Describe the first ten seconds someone spends with it.
- What's on the screen, the page, or in the room? What happens first, and what next? [IU]
- Is it used every day, once a week, or once ever?

## 4. References
- What's the closest thing out there? What does it get right, and what's wrong with it?
- Name a product, film, place, or object that has the feeling you're after.
- Is there a screenshot, link, or example you keep coming back to?

## 5. Edges
- If you're saying yes to this, what are you saying no to? [CH]
- What would make you hate the result, even if it technically works?
- Would it be a mistake to leave X out of the first version? [NS: easy to answer "no"]
- Which of these is it *not*? Offer three plausible ways it could be misread. [ER]

## 6. Success
- How will you know it's right, within ten seconds?
- How will you judge the first version?
- A month from now, what tells you it worked?

## Constraints
- What's already decided: tools, brand, platform, budget, deadline?
- What has to keep working exactly as it does now?
- Who else has a say? Will they see it differently? [ER]

## Wrap-up and check on the interview
- Is there anything about this I'd never guess? [NS: black swans]
- Am I asking the right things? What should I be asking that I'm not? [ER]
- Here's what I heard. What's wrong or missing? Aim for "that's right." [NS]

## Modes by kind of problem

Add questions from the matching mode on top of the six areas.

**Software feature**
- Where in the product does this live? What triggers it?
- What should happen when it fails, or when there's no data?
- Which existing behaviour must not change?

**UI / visual design**
- Place it on each of these, 1 to 5: dense or roomy, playful or serious, loud or quiet.
- Light, dark, or both? Is there a brand colour or an existing design system?
- What should motion feel like: snappy, soft, or none?
- Offer two or three contrasting sketches and ask which is closest.

**API / developer tool**
- Show me the call you wish you could write. Pseudocode is fine.
- What should the error look like when someone gets it wrong?
- Should the defaults be safe, or fast?

**Data / backend / architecture**
- What are the nouns? Which ones does the user actually care about?
- What has to be instant, and what can be slow? What volumes are we talking about?
- What happens to the data when someone leaves?

**Writing / content / copy**
- Who's reading, and what's on their mind when they get to it?
- What should they do or feel when they finish?
- Which three words should they remember?
- Show me writing you admire, and writing you can't stand.

**Product / strategy**
- Who wins if this works? Who loses?
- What would have to be true for this to be a bad idea?
- What's the smallest version that would teach you whether it's right?

**Research / analysis**
- What decision will this inform? What would change your mind?
- What do you already believe the answer is?
- How deep is deep enough? Which sources do you trust, and which don't you?

**Personal decisions / planning**
- What are the options as you see them today?
- Picture yourself a year from now, glad you chose. What did you choose?
- Whose opinion matters here, and whose shouldn't?

## Follow-up patterns

| You hear | You do |
|---|---|
| A vague answer | "Can you give me an example?" [MT] |
| A solution ("add a button") | "What would that let you do?" [MT] |
| "Always" / "usually" / "would" | "When was the last time? What happened?" [MT] |
| "Everything" | "If half had to go, which half?" |
| "I'll know it when I see it" | Offer 2 or 3 contrasting mini-sketches and ask which is closest. |
| An emotional word ("annoying", "magical") | Label it: "It sounds like ___ really bothers you." [NS] |
| A rich answer | Mirror the key words, or say "Say more about that." [IU, NS] |
| A conflict with an earlier answer | "Earlier X, now Y. Which wins when they clash?" |
| A word with two meanings | Show both readings and let them pick. [ER] |

# Question bank

Pick questions from here. Don't ask them all. Rephrase each one using the user's own words and what you found in the repo.

## 1. Core
- In one sentence, what is this? (Offer your own draft for them to correct.)
- Who is it for? Who is it definitely not for?
- What does that person do today instead? What hurts about it?
- If you could only ship one thing, what would it be?

## 2. Picture
- Walk me through the moment someone first runs into this.
- What's on the screen? What do they click, type, or see first?
- What does it feel like when it's working? Give me a real moment, not an adjective.
- Is it something people use every day, once a week, or once ever?

## 3. References
- What's the closest thing that already exists? What does it get right? What's wrong with it?
- Name a product, film, place, or object that has the feeling you're after.
- Is there a screenshot, a link, or a competitor you keep thinking about?

## 4. Edges
- What would make you hate the result, even if it technically works?
- What's tempting to add but should stay out of version 1?
- Which of these is it NOT: [offer 3 plausible misreadings]?

## 5. Constraints
- What's already decided (stack, brand, platform, deadline)?
- What must keep working exactly as it does now?
- Who else has a say in this?

## 6. Success
- How will you judge the first version?
- What would make you say "yes, that's it" within ten seconds?
- What number or behaviour would show it's working a month from now?

## Variants by kind of work

**UI / visual**
- Should it be dense or roomy? Playful or serious? Loud or quiet? (Ask them to place it on each scale, 1 to 5.)
- Light, dark, or both? Is there a brand colour?
- What should the motion feel like: snappy, soft, or none?

**API / developer tool**
- Show me the call you wish you could write. Pseudocode is fine.
- What should the error look like when someone gets it wrong?
- Should the defaults be safe or fast?

**Data / backend**
- What are the nouns? Which ones does the user actually care about?
- What has to be instant? What can be slow?
- What happens to the data when someone leaves?

**Copy / content**
- Who's reading, and what's on their mind when they read it?
- Which three words should someone remember afterwards?
- Show me writing you admire, and writing you can't stand.

## Patterns for following up

- **Vague answer** → "Can you give me an example of that?"
- **Sounds like a solution** → "What problem does that solve? Is there another way?"
- **"Everything"** → "If you had to cut half, which half goes?"
- **"I'll know it when I see it"** → Offer 2 or 3 contrasting mini-sketches and ask which is closest.
- **Conflicting answers** → "Earlier you said X, now Y. Which wins when they clash?"

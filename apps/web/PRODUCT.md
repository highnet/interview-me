# PRODUCT.md

## Register
A developer tool landing page. It's calm and literate, and it's confident without hype.

## Users
People who work with coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI and others) and have ideas that run ahead of their words. Agents also read the site: `/agents` and `/skill.md` are written for them.

## Product purpose
An agent skill that interviews the user until it can say the idea back and get "that's right", then writes a brief and builds from it.

## Positioning
Ask first, then build. The method is grounded in five books on interviewing, and the site cites them.

## Conversion & proof
- Primary action: copy `npx interview-me install`.
- Proof: the interactive transcript demo, the cited sources, and support for 10 agents.

## Brand personality
Curious, warm, brief. A good interviewer, not a form.

## Anti-references
- Purple gradients.
- Grids of icon cards.
- "Supercharge your workflow" copy.
- Fake testimonials.

## Design principles
1. The interview is the hero. Show a conversation, don't describe one.
2. One accent, used like a red pen.
3. Editorial type: serif headlines, sans body, mono for anything an agent or terminal would say.

## Accessibility & inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Demo answers are real buttons.
- Tabs use roles.
